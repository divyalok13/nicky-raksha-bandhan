A LETTER FROM GOLU

Dear Nicky 💌

I've never been very good at saying these things out loud, so maybe this little website can say them for me.

A lot of people come into my life when they need something from me. But you have always felt different.

You don't need a reason to be there.

Whenever I've felt lonely, you've somehow been there — without conditions, without expecting anything back, just because I'm your brother. And honestly, that means more to me than I probably ever tell you.

You have this habit of keeping your pain to yourself. Even when you're hurting, you sometimes choose to deal with it alone. And that's probably one of the few things about you that genuinely annoys me.

Not because I don't understand you.

Because I want you to understand that you don't have to do that anymore.

You have a strong brother now.

You don't have to carry every problem alone. You don't have to pretend you're okay when you're not. Whenever life gets too heavy, you can put some of that weight on me.

And if there are ever moments when I make you feel like I wasn't there when you needed me, I'm sorry.

I mean that.

I'll try my best to never let that happen again.

And yes... we fight. 😂

Most of the time, you say sorry first — which is understandable because you're usually wrong anyway. 😌

Okay, okay... sometimes I'm wrong too.

But there's one thing I know for sure.

Even if you ignore me for days, even if we have a stupid fight, even if there's a whole universe between us...

I'll come back.

Every time.

Because if our relationship were a star, I don't think distance could make it disappear.

Maybe we'd move through different galaxies. Maybe there would be days when we couldn't see each other clearly. Maybe life would pull us in completely different directions.

But some things have their own gravity.

And you will always have that kind of gravity in my life.

So if one day we're far apart, remember this:

No amount of distance will be enough to make me stop coming back to you.

I'll come back with zero ego.

I'll sit down.

We'll talk.

We'll clear everything.

And then we'll probably start fighting again over your clothes and my stuff. 😂

Because that's us.
